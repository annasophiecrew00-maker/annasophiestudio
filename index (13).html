The Studio

Anna Sophie Studio

I'm Anna Sophie, a professional dancer and choreographer based in Geneva, Switzerland. I create choreographed dance content for musicians, brands, and creators of every genre and every size.

For over 9 years, I have been turning sound into movement. From indie musicians releasing their debut single to established brands launching global campaigns, my work is to find the dance that lives inside the music and bring it to life on screen.

Every commission is approached as a collaboration. Your vision, my craft, your audience, mine — together we create content that moves people and grows your reach.
